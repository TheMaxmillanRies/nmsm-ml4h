Third-party notices

The package contains the study's implementations and adapters, not a claim that
every baseline is official author code.

- SurvNODE: algorithm/code-informed recurrent-graph adaptation; upstream reference
  https://github.com/stefangroha/SurvNODE . The upstream checkout inspected for the
  study did not include a top-level license. No upstream repository, notebooks,
  data or pretrained checkpoints are bundled. This package does not purport to
  grant rights to upstream material.
- MixMarkov: https://github.com/spotify-research/mixmarkov . The study implements
  its generalized-Dirichlet likelihood using PyTorch derivatives and SciPy
  Newton-CG. The upstream Apache-2.0 license is retained in
  `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/MixMarkov-Apache-2.0.txt`.
- IDNetwork and msPseudo: paper-based reimplementations with graph/forecasting
  adaptations described in the manuscript; not official code.
- msCox: Python implementation of transition-specific Cox and Aalen–Johansen
  estimators, not redistributed `mstate` R code.
- MIMIC-IV-derived respiratory data: Moukheiber et al. (2025), version 1.1.0,
  DOI 10.13026/wewp-sj67. Obtain data independently under PhysioNet's terms.
